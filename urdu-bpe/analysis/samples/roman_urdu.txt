Aaj subah se barish ho rahi hai aur sab log ghar mein hain. Ammi ne pakoray banaye aur chai ke saath hum ne purani filmein dekhin. Mera chhota bhai keh raha tha ke kal school band hoga kyunke sarkon par pani khara hai. Main ne usay samjhaya ke abhi koi announcement nahi hui, isliye pehle homework khatam kar lo. Shaam ko Ali ka message aya ke cricket match cancel ho gaya hai, magar woh phir bhi milne aayega. Sach poocho to mujhe barish bohat pasand hai, lekin bijli ka masla har dafa pareshan karta hai. Pichle hafte teen din tak light nahi thi aur phone charge karna bhi mushkil ho gaya tha. Insha Allah agle mahine hum Murree jayenge, agar mausam theek raha. Abbu kehte hain ke pehle gari ki service karwa lo, warna raste mein koi masla ho sakta hai. Khair, ab main so raha hoon, kal subah jaldi uthna hai aur daftar waqt par pohanchna hai.
