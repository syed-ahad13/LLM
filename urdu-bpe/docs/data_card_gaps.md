- <!-- REVIEW --> **Genre balance.** {top_genre} is the largest genre at {top_genre_share} of the text ({genre_shares}). Wikipedia is kept whole ({share_wiki_ur}), so encyclopedic register is over-represented next to everyday Urdu. FineWeb-2 is capped at 45% and subsampled (rate {rate_fineweb2_urd}), and UFAL is subsampled (rate {rate_ufal_umc}). Urdu News 1M lost its sentence punctuation and some letters at source (اسلام آباد stored as اسلام باد), so it contributes only {share_urdu_news_1m}.
- <!-- REVIEW --> **Time periods.** The FineWeb-2 text comes from one of its two files, so it covers Common Crawl snapshots CC-MAIN-2021-10 to 2024-18 only; the 2013–2021 crawls are in the file we did not download. UFAL was released in 2014, and its sentences are shuffled, so they carry no document context. VOA Urdu articles date from {voa_range}, Hum Sub posts from {humsub_range}, and the Wikipedia dump is from 2026-10-01. CC-100 (2018 crawls) was not used.
- <!-- REVIEW --> **Script.** Only Urdu in Arabic script is kept. Roman Urdu is excluded by design: lines mostly in other scripts are dropped unless they hold Urdu function words. Expect the tokenizer to handle Roman Urdu poorly.
- <!-- REVIEW --> **Religious and poetic text.** The line filter removed {arabic_persian_lines} lines ({arabic_persian_mb} MB) as Arabic or Persian, mostly Quran and hadith quotations and Persian verse, and {sindhi_docs} whole Sindhi documents ({sindhi_mb} MB of Sindhi lines, including the Sindhi pages in our VOA crawl). Religious and classical poetic writing is therefore under-represented. Short unvowelled Arabic quotations inside Urdu sentences remain.
- <!-- REVIEW --> **Bot-generated stubs.** Many Wikipedia articles are template stubs made by bots (asteroids, railway stations, census places, footballers): 9 of 20 sampled articles in Step 3. Boilerplate removal took their shared headings (the asteroid heading was in 26,587 articles), but the template sentences stay, so these patterns are over-represented. No near-duplicate detection was run.
- <!-- REVIEW --> **ه (U+0647).** Normalization maps every Arabic heh to gol heh ہ ({heh_per_million} per million characters in the Step 4 audit samples). Where a writer typed ه inside a word for do-chashmi heh (بهی for بھی), the result is a misspelling (بہی), not the intended letter.
- <!-- REVIEW --> **Regional variety.** Most sources are Pakistani: VOA Urdu's Pakistan coverage, Hum Sub, Makhzan, and the outlets of Urdu News 1M. Of FineWeb's documents in the corpus, {fineweb_pk} have a .pk domain and {fineweb_in} an .in domain ({fineweb_com} .com), so Indian Urdu is under-represented; the domain is only a rough proxy. Punjabi and Saraiki written in Shahmukhi share the script and can pass the language filter: {shahmukhi_docs} documents contain ݨ (U+0768), a letter of those languages and not of Urdu. Other leakage is not measured.
