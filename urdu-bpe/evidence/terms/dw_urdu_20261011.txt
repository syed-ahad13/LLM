# Terms snapshot for DW Urdu (dw_urdu), collect/scrape/probe.py, 2026-10-11
The probe's first terms-link finder picked a news section page (https://www.dw.com/ur/انسانی-حقوق/s-60947758),
not a terms page; that snapshot was removed. DW states its terms in its robots.txt
(evidence/robots/dw_urdu_20261011.txt):
  "DW content is made available for your personal, non-commercial use. Use of any device, tool, or process
  designed to data mine or scrape the content using automated means is prohibited without prior written
  permission from Deutsche Welle."
DW was rejected on that basis and received no further requests.
